## MessageFormat

- **Author:** Jeff Hansen ([@jeffijoe](https://github.com/jeffijoe))
- **Project:** https://github.com/jeffijoe/messageformat.net
- **Package:** [MessageFormat](https://www.nuget.org/packages/MessageFormat) 8.0.0
- **License:** MIT
- **Used by:** `FluentLocalizer` (core package), and through it every other package, the samples and the website

MessageFormat parses and formats every FluentLocalizer template written in the [ICU message syntax](https://unicode-org.github.io/icu/userguide/format_parse/messages/): placeholders, `plural`, `select` and the other ICU constructs it supports.
